02 / DOCUMENTATION

How Veritype works

1. Plan and extraction

Local Ollama extracts literal decisions from your plan. You review and can correct them. Extraction never creates cultural evidence.

2. Entity verification

Qloo search returns possible entities. You select the correct show or IP. The server checks your choice against real Qloo search results.

3. Ranking and integrity

Qloo ranks named brand candidates for the selected cultural signal. Veritype checks derived ranks, affinity values, entity IDs and available provenance against the returned evidence.

4. Challenge and repair

If a proposed brand ranks below an alternative, Veritype can explain the comparison. Only your explicit approval triggers a fresh re-test.

5. Evidence boundaries

Qloo affinity describes relative cultural signals within the supplied candidate set. It cannot establish sales, causal lift, licensing rights, sponsorship success, or an individual's behavior.

6. Current limitations

Brand comparisons are live; music, dining and venue workflows are not yet implemented. Sessions are temporary and server-local. This is a development application, not a hardened public deployment.
